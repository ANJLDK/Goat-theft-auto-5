GoatDriver (optional)
Goat Simulator: PAYDAY style driving. R gets you in and out of a car, the goat clamps onto the
steering wheel and flops out of the window, Shift boosts, left mouse jumps the car.
Install: copy GoatDriver.cs and GoatDriver.ini into GTA V's scripts folder (next to GoatTheftAuto.cs).
Without it you enter vehicles the normal GTA way (F).
Tuning the seat position: see the comments at the top of GoatDriver.cs (NumPad keys in game).
