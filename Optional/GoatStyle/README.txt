GoatStyle (optional)
Trick score and combos, Goat Sim HUD and popups, mutators (F11), WASTED/BUSTED bonus screen,
achievements (F8), photo mode with hats and filters (F7), hold F for slow motion.
Install: copy GoatStyle.cs and GoatStyle.ini into GTA V's scripts folder (next to GoatTheftAuto.cs).
Remove: delete those two files (and GoatStyle.save if you want to reset achievements).
